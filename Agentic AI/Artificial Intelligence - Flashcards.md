#flashcards/agentic-ai/artificial-intelligence

How is AI defined in the rational-agent view?
?
Designing agents that choose actions expected to maximize a performance measure, given their percepts and knowledge.

What is an agent (Russell & Norvig)?
?
Anything that perceives its environment through sensors and acts on it through actuators.

What are the four main paradigms of AI?
?
Symbolic AI, statistical machine learning, deep learning, and reinforcement learning.

What was the main weakness of symbolic expert systems?
?
The knowledge bottleneck and brittleness: rules had to be written by hand and failed on new inputs.

Which 2017 paper introduced the Transformer?
?
"Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al.).

What is the difference between narrow AI and AGI?
?
Narrow AI is competent at specific tasks. AGI is hypothetical broad, human-level competence with no agreed definition.

How does agentic AI relate to the classic agent loop?
?
It is the perceive–decide–act loop with an LLM as the policy, plus tools and memory.

What is the "AI effect"?
?
Once a technique works reliably, people stop calling it AI.
